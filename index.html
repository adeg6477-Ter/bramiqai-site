The reports

One record of the site. Three reports for three decisions.

Each report states its method, its assumptions, and how complete the data was.

Water, energy and carbon record

An hour-by-hour account of what a site consumed, with evaporative, closed-loop, and air-cooled designs compared on the same workload.

Decision it supports
Permits, council hearings, and state water disclosures.
What is in the report
  • Electricity used, hour by hour, from GPU-level readings
  • Water use, calculated with the federal cooling tower formula
  • Carbon dioxide from grid electricity, at the EPA rate for the region
  • Every assumption listed, so a reviewer can challenge it

Independent verification report

When a site is asked to cut its load, this report states whether it did, by how much, and how certain the answer is.

Decision it supports
Whether a curtailment commitment was honored.
What is in the report
  • A verdict: met, met within baseline variability, or not met
  • Baseline and actual power, with a chart of the event
  • A range for the result, so normal swings are not mistaken for a reduction
  • A data check that withholds the number when readings are missing

GPU asset record

For each GPU: time spent at high temperature and the number of thermal cycles. It records what happened and makes no prediction about remaining life.

Decision it supports
The condition of hardware that secures a loan.
What is in the report
  • Hours at or above a set temperature, per GPU
  • Thermal cycles: full swings from cool to hot
  • Energy delivered through each GPU
  • The fleet ranked by how hard each GPU was run
How it works

From raw readings to a record someone else can check.

1

Collect, read-only

A small collector reads GPU power and temperature on your machines. It queries values and sends no commands.

2

Add public context

Readings are combined with open data for the site: weather observations, grid prices, and the grid's emissions rate.

3

Report, with the working shown

Every figure traces back to a reading or a stated assumption. Where a number is calculated and not metered, the report says so.
